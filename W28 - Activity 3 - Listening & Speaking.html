Phase 3 — Ask Arthur

Imagine you can interview Arthur about his first job. Write 4 questions. Every question starts with a question word — and his story is in the past.

📐 Past questions — the structure

Question word+did+he / she / you+base verb…?
✓ Where did you study?
✓ What did she buy?
✗ Where you studied?
✗ What did she bought?
💡 After did, the verb goes back to its base form: did … go (not went), did … carry (not carried).

Some possible questions — and Arthur's answers:
  • When did he start his first job? → In 1994, when he was in high school.
  • How often did he deliver the newspapers? → Every Wednesday, after school.
  • What did he use to carry the newspapers? → A trolley.
  • Where did he put the newspapers? → In people's letter boxes.
  • What was the weather like? → Rain, hail, and hot 30-degree days.
Your questions don't need to be the same — check the question word, did, and the base verb.

Phase 4 — Your First Job

Now it's your story. Then you ask Ali about hers.

🎙️ Your voice note — 60 seconds

Part 2 — Ask Ali 2 questions about her first job. Start each one with a question word.
