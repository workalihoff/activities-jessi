Part 1 — Written Report
Write the incident report
Think of a real or realistic situation where a payment or process failed at your bank. Write a short, structured incident report in 4 sections.
Your scenario — use a real one or adapt this

A client submitted an ACH payment on Monday. The payment was rejected because the beneficiary account number was incorrect. The client was not notified until Tuesday morning. The issue was escalated to the operations team and resolved by end of day Tuesday. The client resubmitted the payment on Wednesday and it was processed successfully.

Grammar reminders
✓ Past passive for what happened: was rejected / was escalated / was notified
✓ Present perfect for current status: has been resolved / has been processed / has been resubmitted
✓ Time expressions go at the end: "...on Monday" / "...by end of day" / "...within 24 hours"

Part 2 — Voice Note
Report it out loud
Now say the same incident out loud — without reading your written report word for word. 60–90 seconds. No script.
How to record & submit
1. Read your written report once to remind yourself of the content.
2. Put it aside — don't read from it when you record.
3. Record yourself reporting the incident out loud (60–90 seconds).
4. Send the voice note on WhatsApp or upload it in Classroom.
Report the incident as if you're giving a verbal update to your supervisor.
Try to use "was rejected," "was escalated," "has been resolved," and at least one vocabulary word from Activity 1 — naturally, without forcing it.
Self-check after recording
  • ✓I used past passive at least once without reading from my notes
  • ✓I kept talking even when I wasn't sure of a word
  • ✓I didn't read my written report out loud
