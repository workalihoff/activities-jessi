Your turn to negotiate.

This is a bigger activity than usual — it replaces the live practice we missed this week.

Recap: ClearVerify's platform went down and delayed your SAT report. Oliver Bennett already told you the root cause (a subcontractor issue) and offered a service credit. Now it's a follow-up call — and this time, you're negotiating for better terms going forward: a guaranteed turnaround time, a written SLA, or stronger compensation.
Part 1 — Plan your negotiation (written)
What do you want to negotiate?
e.g. a faster guaranteed turnaround time, a formal written SLA, a bigger service credit
What's your opening line?
How will you start the conversation? Use language from the listening activity.
What's your walk-away point?
What's the minimum you'd accept before this stops being a good deal?
Useful phrases to use
I'd like to discuss… what we're looking for is… could we agree on…? we can go with… I'm happy to accept… I look forward to working with you
Part 2 — The call (spoken, 3–5 minutes)
3–5
minutes · no script
1Open the call — state what you want to discuss and why.
2Make your case — explain why this matters (the SAT deadline, the impact on your team).
3Handle pushback — imagine Oliver offers something smaller than what you asked for. Respond — hold firm, or find a middle ground.
4Close professionally — accept, or agree on next steps, and end on a positive note.
Tip: you can glance at your Part 1 notes, but don't read them word for word — the goal is natural, spontaneous speech, like we practice in live sessions.
How to submit your recording
🎙️Option A — WhatsApp: Record a voice note directly in WhatsApp (hold the microphone icon) and send it to Ali. This works even for longer recordings like this one.
💻Option B — Online recorder: If you'd rather record on your computer, use a free tool like vocaroo.com — no account needed, just record and copy the link it gives you. Paste that link in the Classroom comment box, or send it on WhatsApp.
