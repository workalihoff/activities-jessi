Student: Jessi · English with Ali

Listen, Read, React

This week's activity is different — a real BBC episode made for English learners, connected to your world: banking and fraud.

Step 1 — Find the episode
BBC Learning English — 6 Minute English: "Fraud and scams"

Go to bbc.co.uk/learningenglish, use the search bar, and type "Fraud and scams 6 Minute English." The page has the audio, a full transcript, and a short glossary — use all three if you need to.

1Listen to the episode once without reading the transcript.
2Listen a second time with the transcript, to check what you understood.
3Come back here and answer the questions below in your own words.
Step 2 — Comprehension questions
Reminder: answer in full sentences — this is also writing practice, not just a memory check.
1. According to the episode, about how much money did criminals steal through fraud and scams in the UK last year (according to UK Finance)?
2. What is "phishing"? Explain it in your own words.
3. What does "to fall for" something mean, according to the programme?
4. Give one example of a scam mentioned in the episode.
5. What advice would you give a colleague to avoid falling for a scam like this?
Step 3 — Voice memo (60–90 seconds, no script)

Record a short voice memo reacting naturally to what you heard — like you're telling a friend about something interesting you learned. Send it the same way you always do (WhatsApp or Classroom audio).

💭 What was the most interesting part of this episode for you?
💭 Has your bank ever dealt with something similar — phishing emails, fraud attempts, suspicious requests?
💭 Do you think banks in Guatemala face the same kind of fraud risks described in the episode?
💭 What would you do if you received a suspicious email asking for account details?
