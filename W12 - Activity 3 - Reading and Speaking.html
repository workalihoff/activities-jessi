Read, React, Respond

This week's activity is different — a real financial news article, connected to your world: cloud outages affecting banks and fintechs.

Step 1 — Read the article
"PayPay and other fintechs suffer fresh AWS outages"

Amazon CloudFront "operational issue" the latest in a series of setbacks for cloud provider

The Banker — Fintech, July 16, 2026. By John Everington, with additional reporting by Kimberley Long.

Link: https://www.thebanker.com/content/bf52cc4b-9e01-4aa8-b62d-f06b838a00ed

1Read the article once for the general idea — don't worry about every word.
2Read it a second time, more carefully, checking the specific details.
3Come back here and answer the questions below in your own words.
Step 2 — Comprehension questions
Reminder: answer in full sentences — this is also writing practice, not just a memory check.
1. Which fintech company is mentioned as having problems because of the AWS outage?
2. What did Amazon say caused the disruption?
3. Is this the first time AWS problems have affected financial companies? Give one earlier example mentioned in the article.
4. How are UK regulators responding to these repeated outages?
5. What new requirements might Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Oracle face as "critical third parties"?
Step 3 — Voice memo (60–90 seconds, no script)

Record a short voice memo reacting naturally to this article — like you're telling a colleague about something you just read. Send it the same way you always do (WhatsApp or Classroom audio).

💭 What was the most interesting part of this article for you?
💭 Do you think it's risky for banks and fintechs to depend so heavily on one cloud provider like AWS? Why or why not?
💭 If your bank's systems depended on a cloud provider that failed, what do you think would happen operationally?
💭 Do you think stricter regulation, like the UK's "critical third party" designation, is a good idea? Why?
