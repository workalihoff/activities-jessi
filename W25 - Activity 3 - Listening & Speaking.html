Phase 1 — Key Vocabulary

Six phrases you'll hear in the episode. Read these first — it'll make the listening much easier.

Feasible
Realistic, doable — possible to actually achieve.
"Does that sound feasible?" — checking if a deadline is realistic for someone.
Firm / hard deadline
A deadline that absolutely cannot move — no flexibility.
"Is this a hard deadline, or is there some flexibility?"
Contingency
Extra time built into a plan in case something goes wrong.
"You want to add in contingency in case someone's out sick."
Pencil in
To tentatively reserve a date, without full confirmation yet.
"Can I pencil in this date and confirm later?"
Ballpark figure
A rough estimate — not exact, just approximately right.
"I just need a ballpark figure for how long this will take."
Close the loop
To confirm something clearly so nobody is left unsure.
"Always reply to confirm — you're closing the loop."
