The Six Spots

Same idea every time: reformulate, don't memorize the fix in isolation.

1
"I hope all doing well,"
Missing auxiliary: "all doing well" needs "are" — this happens whenever a sentence needs BE as a helper verb, not just as part of a passive.
"I hope you're all doing well,"
2
"The first activity is review that..."
Missing "to": after "is" describing what an activity involves, English needs the infinitive — "is TO review," not "is review."
"The first activity is to review whether..."
3
"review that all vendors complete to sign"
Unnatural verb pattern: "complete to sign" isn't a real English structure. When something is finished, English usually just uses the simple verb, or "have + participle."
"confirm that all vendors have signed"
4
"The second activity is I will review and complete the reconciliations and sending in the system."
Mixed verb forms: "review," "complete" (base form) and "sending" (-ing) are all doing the same job in this sentence — they need to match. Also, "is I will..." mixes two structures. Pick one: either "is + -ing" for all three, or restructure with "I will."
"The second activity is reviewing, completing, and sending the reconciliations in the system."
5
"Vinicio is accountable for doing the ACH Batch transactions and sends me for the review"
Same parallel-form issue: "doing" and "sends" don't match — after "accountable for," everything needs to stay in the -ing form. Also, "sends me" is missing an object — send WHAT to me?
"Vinicio is accountable for doing the ACH Batch transactions and sending them to me for review"
6
"Please if you have any questions, let me know."
Word order: this is the second time this exact pattern has shown up (same structure as the Oliver email a couple of weeks ago) — "please" goes with the main instruction, not at the very front before "if."
"Please let me know if you have any questions."

The Bigger Picture

Four throughlines connect these six spots — worth knowing, not just fixing case by case.

Auxiliary / verb-form droppingConfirmed — ongoing

"I hope all doing well" joins "haven't respond," "has been...applied," and "will completed" — the common thread is a verb missing its correct supporting form, whatever that form should be (BE, HAVE, or a plain future form).

Missing "to" before an infinitive complementNew

"The first activity is review..." — after "is" describing a role or task, English usually needs "to + verb." Worth watching for more instances before treating it as fully confirmed.

Parallel verb forms in listsNew

When listing more than one action for the same subject, all the verbs need to match in form — all base form, or all -ing. Two clear instances in this one email (spots 4 and 5).

"Please if..." word orderConfirmed — 2nd instance

Same exact structure as the email to Oliver a couple of weeks ago. This one's ready for a dedicated moment in a future session — not random anymore, it's a pattern.

Full Stronger Version — For Reference

Hi Vinicio, Priya,
 
I hope you're all doing well.
 
While Ricardo is away, we need to complete three important activities, and I'll be overseeing their completion.
 
The first activity is to confirm we don't have any more questions from our vendor about the new policy, and to confirm that all vendors have signed. I'm delegating this to Priya. The deadline is this week.
 
The second activity is reviewing, completing, and sending the reconciliations in the system — I'll take care of this myself.
 
For the third activity, Vinicio is accountable for doing the ACH Batch transactions and sending them to me for review. I'll then send them to Andrea. The deadline for this task is tomorrow.
 
Please let me know if you have any questions.
 
Regards,
Jessi
