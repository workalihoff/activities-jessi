The shape, again

1
Headline — the one thing that matters most, first.
2
Key detail — the one fact that explains or supports the headline.
3
Next step — what happens next, and when.

New this time: what if there's more than one issue?

In our session, every update was about one thing. Real work usually isn't that clean. When you have two things to report, the 3-Line structure still works — you just have to choose one as the headline and treat the other as something you mention only if asked, or save for a second, separate update.

🎯 This week's stretch

Ask yourself: "If Ricardo only remembers one sentence from this, which one should it be?" That's your headline. Everything else — even if it's also true and also matters — waits its turn.

⚠️ Two ways this goes wrong

1) Cramming both issues into the headline — it stops being a headline and becomes a run-on.
2) Leading with backstory instead of the headline — "So first this happened, then that happened, and..." — that's the structure we're trying to avoid.
